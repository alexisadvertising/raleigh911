The Raleigh County Emergency Services is proud of its involvement with the community.

- Lectures to the Girl Scouts of America about 911 and safety.
- Presentations are often given to schools, civic organizations and church groups.
- Tours of the 911/EOC can be arranged by calling [304-255-0911](tel:+13042550911) or by filling out a brief request form.
