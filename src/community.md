The Raleigh County Emergency Services is proud of its involvement with the community. Our work does not stop at answering 911 calls. We also train, plan and respond alongside our partners across Raleigh County.

## Visits, talks and tours

- Lectures to the Girl Scouts of America about 911 and safety.
- Presentations are often given to schools, civic organizations and church groups.
- Tours of the 911/EOC can be arranged by calling [304-255-0911](tel:+13042550911) or by filling out a brief request form.

## Disaster response and supplies

When flooding, fires or other disasters strike, Emergency Management helps coordinate the response and gets supplies to the people who need them.

![Smoke and flames rising from the opening of a tunnel in a wooded hillside.](/images/coal-city-tunnel-fire-sept-2026.jpg)

**September 2026: Coal City tunnel fire.** Responders were called to a fire at a tunnel opening in Coal City.

![A forklift loading a pallet of bottled water onto a truck, with an Emergency Management vehicle beside it.](/images/disaster-supplies-loading.jpg)

**Disaster supplies.** Emergency Management staff load bottled water and other supplies for communities affected by flooding and other emergencies.

![A staff member on a forklift moving a bale of shredded paper in a warehouse bay.](/images/forklift-training-may-2026.jpg)

**May 2026: forklift training.** Staff train on the equipment used to move and load disaster supplies.

## Training and exercises

Practice makes a real response faster and safer. We take part in exercises with first responders, hospitals and schools so everyone is ready before an emergency happens.

![Fire engines and responders at a practice exercise with an overturned bus beside a football field.](/images/liberty-bus-wreck-exercise.jpg)

**Liberty High School bus wreck exercise.** A practice response to a bus crash with multiple patients.

![Responders and hospital staff in a hospital hallway with a patient on a stretcher during a practice exercise.](/images/raleigh-general-mass-casualty-exercise.jpg)

**Raleigh General Hospital mass casualty exercise.** Hospital staff and responders practice caring for many patients at once.

![Responders in safety vests moving a patient on a stretcher past a decontamination tent at a hospital entrance.](/images/raleigh-general-hazmat-exercise.jpg)

**Raleigh General Hospital hazmat exercise.** Practice for a hazardous materials emergency, with decontamination at the hospital entrance. To learn more about hazardous materials planning, see the [LEPC page](/lepc/).
