## What is the LEPC?

The LEPC (Local Emergency Planning Committee) is responsible for developing and planning an emergency response to accidents involving hazardous materials. With membership representatives from both the private and public sectors, evacuation routes, identified hazardous chemical facilities and available shelters are compiled into a plan. The response plan can be activated if needed.

Visit our [downloads section](/downloads/) to get a copy of the Hazardous Materials Annex, the Hazardous Materials Incident Report or the Shelter in Place brochure.

## Community Right to Know

The LEPC functions under the "Community Right to Know" amendment. A resident may request information on specific hazardous materials. Write to the LEPC Coordinator, Department of Emergency Services, 162 Industrial Park Rd, Beaver, WV 25813, subject to the "Community Right to Know" reporting under the Superfund Amendments and Reauthorization Act (SARA) Title III. You can also email [lepc@raleighcounty.gov](mailto:lepc@raleighcounty.gov).
