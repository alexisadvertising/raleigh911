The Raleigh County Emergency Services Authority recognizes the public's right to access government records in accordance with the West Virginia Freedom of Information Act (W. Va. Code §29B-1-1 et seq.).

Members of the public may submit requests to inspect or obtain copies of records maintained by the Communications Center, subject to applicable laws and exemptions. Records are only released in accordance with FOIA statutes or by valid Subpoena issued by a court of competent jurisdiction.

**Pursuant to WV Code §24-6-13 (C), records are only required to be maintained for a total of two (2) years.**

## What records can be requested?

Depending on availability and applicable legal restrictions, records may include:

- 911 call recordings.
- Dispatch and radio communications recordings.
- Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) incident records.
- Specific reports, if available.

Some records or portions of records may be withheld or redacted when protected by state or federal law.

## How to submit a request

To help us process your request efficiently, please submit your request using one of the following options:

1. **By email.** Include:
    - Your name and contact information.
    - A detailed description of the records being requested.
    - The date, approximate time, and location of the incident, if applicable.
    - Any incident or call reference number, if available.
    - Requests should describe the records being sought as specifically as possible.
2. **By completing the [FOIA Request form](/files/foia-request-form.pdf)** in its entirety and then emailing it to the email address below.

Requests for records maintained by the Raleigh County Emergency Services Authority must be submitted to the Records Custodian in writing. **FOIA requests will not be accepted by phone.**

### Submission information

Email: [raleigh911@raleigh911.org](mailto:raleigh911@raleigh911.org)

Mailing address: Raleigh County Emergency Services Authority, Attn: Records Custodian, 162 Industrial Park Road, Beaver, WV 25813

## What happens after I submit a request?

Upon receipt of a request, the records custodian will review the request and determine whether the requested records are available for release. Reasonable fees will apply for the actual cost of reproducing records, as permitted by law. Any applicable costs will be communicated before records are provided.

**Please note, records are not released electronically. All records must be picked up in person or mailed to the requestor via certified mail. Applicable fees will apply for postage.**

Please note that submitting a request does not guarantee that all requested information can be released. Each request is reviewed in accordance with applicable public records laws and confidentiality requirements.
